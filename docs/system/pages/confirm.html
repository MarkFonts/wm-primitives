The confirm

:active acknowledges a press. When it worked the control confirms it: its press look goes off for 80ms, on for 80ms, then it settles into the result — like a macOS menu item blinking. One keyframe set (motion.css), one call (wmConfirm(el), confirm.js), three recipes: a box blinks its fill and edge, a mark its FILL/HDR paint, a stepper its chevron light. GESTURES.md §14.
