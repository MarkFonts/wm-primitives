Toast toast()

One line of news in a corner, with at most one thing to do. It adds no look to the system: the ground is --surface-hi, the surface the contract names for menus and popovers, with the one hairline, the house corner, and a cast shadow that is black in both themes. The action is 06 Menu with its mark, and the dismiss is 06’s icon-only form, so 03 Rule never shares the plate.

It rests top right on a desktop and at the bottom on a phone, full width, near the thumb. That is CSS, not an option; a host clears its own chrome with --toast-top and --toast-bottom. Every toast leaves after --dur-hint, three seconds, unless it persists, and the clock stops while the pointer or focus is inside it. The action blinks its confirm, then runs, then the toast goes. One at a time; a toast with the same id updates in place.
