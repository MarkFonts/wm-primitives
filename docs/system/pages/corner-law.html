The corner law

A rounded rectangle meets its straight edge at a curvature break: the cap is bending at 1/r, the side at zero, and the eye reads the seam even when it cannot name it. superellipse(1.2) eases curvature to zero before the join instead — G2 continuity, which is how Cal Sans itself is drawn. The UI rounds the way the letterforms do.

The parameter is not the exponent. CSS folds superellipse(1) to round and superellipse(2) to squircle, so the real exponent is 2k — k 1.2 is ≈ 2.30, just past a circle. Scope below is ReCal only, the COSS kit excluded: of 168 corners, 87 take the law, 13 are circles carved out, and 32 sit in the frozen App.css. Live surface: 42.
