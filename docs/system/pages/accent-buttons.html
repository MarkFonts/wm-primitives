Accent buttons AccentButton

Two acid buttons, each a pill (the Download button) or a chip (a Try-bar toggle that is on). The light is a pre-rendered, dithered image behind a near-black face, never a CSS gradient or blur, so it keeps its long falloff without banding. Q5 is underlit: a rim along the bottom edge, a spill beneath it, and a light that deepens slowly on hover while the face never brightens. Q6 to N4 rests as a pinprick of rim light and, on hover, moves the light from beneath to behind: it travels along the bottom edge, round the lower corners and up the sides, and the top edge lights all at once.

Disabled is unlit and neutral, a dark face with a faint hairline and a grey label, never a dimmed acid. A pressed button sits one pixel down. The pill is a full render at its own size, 225 × 32 for Q5 and 227 × 33 for Q6 to N4, so its box is fixed and the host places it. The chip is 32 tall with an 8px radius, and its light is a 9-slice of two end pieces and a stretched middle, so one set serves any width. Dark ground only: the face is --surface.

Q5 has an HDR light. On an HDR display, in the dark theme, its spill, its rim, and its label go past SDR white: each lit layer is a PQ image whose value is the light itself, floor plus glow, peaking at 589 nits, and the label is acid at that same peak, painted through the glyphs. On an SDR screen it is exactly the button above. The images bake the #1a1a1a ground they sit on. In the light theme Q5 is ink on paper: an ink face, an apple light below it, and an apple label. On an HDR display the light theme's Q5 is lit in acid instead, a broad light that peaks at 405 nits on the white ground, with an acid label at 605.

Q6 to N4 has the same HDR light, dark and light: the spill, the travelling rim, the streak, and the label, whose resting ink thins to show acid as the light comes on. The travelling light steps one frame at a time instead of cross-fading. Both looks show their HDR only on an HDR display.

To contain the light, give its box overflow: clip. Never a mask, overflow: hidden on a scroller, opacity, or transform on an ancestor. The pill in a rail above is ReCal's Download, at the foot of its rail.

<AccentButton look="q5" shape="pill">Download</AccentButton>, or look="q6n4" shape="chip". The images are in assets/accent/, and tools/accent/ renders them.
