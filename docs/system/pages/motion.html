The durations motion.css

Every transition reads a token; a duration spelled out in a rule is a lint failure. The values are ReCal’s, written down in its spec, not the fallbacks that had accumulated beside them.

TokenValueFor
--dur-fast140msa state change on one element: hover, press, focus, a colour
--dur-med240mssomething resizing or revealing: a collapse, a panel
--dur-layout300msa large surface moving: the sidebar, the mobile sheet
--dur-slow400mssomething crossing the screen
--dur-hint3snot motion: how long a one-time hint stays
--dur-confirm80msone phase of the confirm; a blink, so off the ladder

Instant, fast, medium

Three steps carry nearly everything. A mark’s rollover runs at --icon-dur, 90ms, under --dur-fast on purpose: at 140 the mark lagged the row it sits in, and a rollover should feel like the pointer, not an animation about it. A state change is fast, 140. A reveal is medium, 240.

The curve --ease

One curve, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1), the Cal Sans case study tester’s unlock curve: quick off the mark, long settle. It was typed out three times with a comment calling it the house easing; it is a token now, --ease in motion.css, and the three read it.

Acknowledge and confirm GESTURES §14

A press is answered twice. :active acknowledges it while the pointer is down. When the press worked, the control’s script adds .wm-confirm: its press look goes off for 80ms, on for 80ms, then the control settles into its result. A blink, steps(1), paint only: no box moves.

t--confirm-offWhat shows
0–80ms1the press look off: the control’s rest paint
80–160ms0the press look on
160ms—the class comes off; the control is in its result state

The morph GESTURES §12

The zoom control opens leftwards out of its mark in one 600ms timeline, 2.5 × --dur-med, and closes in 300, the open folded back. Layout jumps to the end state at once; only the drawing travels.

Transform, opacity, clip only

The morph animates three properties and nothing else, so no frame reflows the page: a magnifier leaves the mark as it fills, zips to where the lozenge will sit, and the rule opens from it by its clip. The theme stack’s stow is a translate, the confirm a custom property that only paint reads.
