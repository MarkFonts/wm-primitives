What is on the lines, and what is not. Text and the space between text blocks are in units of 3px. A chip's padding, a button's, a card's top and bottom, stay on the pad scale (4 6 8 12 16 24 32 48 64) — components keep their own spacing; the column of text keeps the rhythm.
